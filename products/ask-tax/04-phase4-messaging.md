# Phase 4: big idea and messaging matrix

Agent 4 output.

---

## 4.1 The big idea

**Headline statement**

> You are not underpaid because you do not ask. You ask more than they do. You are being charged for asking.

**Core belief shift**

From: *I need to be more confident and finally speak up.*
To: *I have been speaking up for nine years. There is a fee attached to it, and I have been paying it quarterly.*

**Emotional hook**

Relief before anger. The first feeling is not rage at anyone, it is the specific relief of finding out you were not the problem. That relief is what makes her keep reading. Anger makes her comment; relief makes her buy.

**Logical justification**

Across large samples, 64% of women and 59% of men report trying to negotiate for promotion or pay. In job-offer negotiations it is 54% of women against 44% of men. The gap persists anyway. What differs is the outcome: when women make the identical assertive request, they are rated less likeable, less hireable and less promotable. The behaviour is the same. The price is not.

**Why it holds up**

Simple enough to repeat in one sentence. Surprising, because it contradicts the entire category. Concrete, because it resolves to a dollar figure in her own file. Credible, because it is published and checkable. Emotional, because it lifts blame. Durable, because it will still be true in 2030.

## 4.2 Direct offer script (targets the 1 to 5% in-market)

**Hook**
> You have an offer open in another tab and you already know you are going to accept it at the number they gave you.

**Body**
> Here is what the research actually says, and it is not what you have been told. Women negotiate more often than men, not less. 64% to 59%. You are not the problem. What happens is that the same assertive ask, from you, gets read as difficult, and it costs you the promotion even when you win the number.
>
> The Ask Tax is the system for making the ask without paying the fee. A 20-minute audit that tells you what you are currently losing per year, in dollars. 14 word-for-word scripts for the raise conversation, the counter-offer, the promo packet and the walk-back. The five-step sequence that lets you make a hard number land without the room turning on you.
>
> $697. You are at $145k median. A 0.48% raise pays for it. Most people who run the audit find they are leaving $20,000 a year on the table.

**CTA**
> Run the audit. It takes 20 minutes and gives you a number. Then decide.

## 4.3 Workhorse angles

High-spend, broad. These absorb the budget.

### W1. The ask tax

*Pain: she believes she is bad at negotiating.*

> Nine years of asking. Three raises, all under the band. You have been told you need more confidence.
>
> You do not have a confidence problem. Women negotiate more often than men do. What you have is a price you are paying for asking that nobody itemised for you. It has a number. Most women at your level find it is somewhere between $18,000 and $40,000 a year.
>
> Twenty minutes to find out what yours is.

### W2. The scope ledger

*Pain: she took on more and got nothing for it.*

> You absorbed two people's scope when they left. You are still in the same band.
>
> Here is the mistake almost everyone makes: you described it as working hard. Your VP heard a status report. They do not pay for effort, they pay for scope, and scope only counts when it is written in their units.
>
> One page. Their vocabulary. Bring it to calibration and watch what changes.

### W3. The walk-back

*Pain: she got a verbal yes that quietly evaporated.*

> He said yes in the room. Then it became "let me see what I can do at the next cycle." Then the cycle came and went.
>
> A verbal agreement that never gets written down is not an agreement, it is a way of ending an uncomfortable conversation. The counter is one email, sent the same day, in his own words.
>
> The exact template is in the playbook.

### W4. Competent and difficult

*Pain: she has been told she is "a lot" or "intense."*

> Someone has described you as intense, or direct, or "a lot." You have thought about it more than they did.
>
> There is a documented trade here: women who behave the way leaders are expected to behave are rated more competent and less likeable, and the likeability score is what the promotion committee actually uses.
>
> You do not fix that by being softer. You fix it by changing what the number is attached to before it enters the room. Five steps. Scripts included.

## 4.4 Niche angles

Low budget each, high combined depth. Each targets a specific dated circumstance.

| # | Circumstance | Micro-script hook |
|---|---|---|
| N1 | Offer in hand, 72-hour window | "The offer expires Friday and you are going to accept the first number. Twenty minutes before you reply." |
| N2 | Promo packet due | "Your packet is due in three weeks and you are writing it like a status report. That is why the last one failed." |
| N3 | Peer promoted past her | "He got the title. You got his workload. There is a name for the gap and it is not performance." |
| N4 | New manager, no history with her | "New manager, month two. Everything you built before he arrived is invisible to him unless you write it down this week." |
| N5 | Pay transparency law just landed in her state | "The band got published. You now know exactly what you are under by. Here is what to do in the next 30 days." |
| N6 | Female superintendent or site lead | "Every new sub on site asks for the guy in charge. You are the guy in charge. That daily tax has a dollar value." |
| N7 | Engineer moving to first management role | "You got the team and no comp change. That is a promotion in title only and it is the most expensive one to accept quietly." |
| N8 | Only woman on the leadership call | "Fourteen people on the call. One of you gets interrupted. There is a script for the re-entry." |
| N9 | Credit taken by a colleague | "He repeated your point nine minutes later and the room agreed with him. You need the written version, not a better tone." |
| N10 | Budget objection already received | "They said there is no budget. There is a band, and the band has a top. Here is the exact response." |
| N11 | Returning from parental leave | "You came back to a reduced scope nobody discussed with you. The first 30 days decide the next three years." |
| N12 | Counter-offer from current employer | "They matched it. Now think about why it took a resignation letter to find that money." |

## 4.5 Vocabulary rules

Write in her language, not marketing language.

| Do not write | Write |
|---|---|
| Boost your confidence | Say the number and stop talking |
| Know your worth | Your band tops out at $168k. You are at $141k |
| Empower yourself | Bring the ledger to calibration |
| Level up | Get out of the band you have been parked in |
| Own your power | Get it in writing the same day |
| Toxic workplace | The room went cold after you asked |
| Smash the glass ceiling | The promo committee scores likeability and nobody told you |
| Unlock your potential | $27,400 a year, compounding |

Real ICP vocabulary to use throughout: band, calibration, promo packet, scope, level, comp cycle, merit increase, skip-level, sign-off, change order, billable, headcount, req, RIF, span of control.

## 4.6 Banned language

Not on moral grounds. Each of these costs money for a specific reason.

| Banned | Cost |
|---|---|
| Men as the antagonist, in any framing | Meta policy strikes, account restriction, and it collapses the Phase 5 scaling protocol |
| "Alpha", "boss babe", "girlboss", "she-EO" | Signals the low-ticket consumer category. Caps your price at $97 |
| Grievance-only hooks with no dated action | Attracts the refund cohort. See the refund sensitivity row in file 03 |
| Income claims presented as typical | Advertising policy exposure and refund liability |
| "Sisterhood", "community of women" | She is not buying a community. She has a meeting on Thursday |

The rule underneath all of it: **the enemy is a mechanism, not a gender.** The ask tax is something being done to her by a system that has a documented shape, and a system can be beaten with a script. A gender cannot, which is why that framing sells nothing at this price point.
